Welcome to Digital Bracket Maker! This is a fast, and simple website to help you organize tournaments and leagues in seconds.

Whether you are hosting a video game tournament, a sports league, or just a friendly competition with friends, this tool handles all the complex scheduling and math for you.


Key Details to focus:

1. No sign up required! No emails or paying!

2. Knockout brackets with easy two-legged matches that can very per round by the click of a check box!

3. Round Robin with automatic standings calculator and a check box for home and away

4. You can add the scores to every match easily just by scrolling down

5. Digital Bracket Maker is still being improved so add as much feedback as you can, so Digital Bracket Maker can be even better

We hope you enjoy Digital Bracket Maker!
